Private utility

Personal Rclone Sync

A private, single-user setup used to transfer files between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive account with rclone.

Purpose

This setup is used for personal file backup, synchronization, and restoration. It is not offered as a service to the public and does not provide accounts for other users.

How Google data is used

Access to Google Drive is used only to list, read, create, update, and delete files when the owner runs an rclone command. Transfers occur directly between the owner's device and Google Drive.
